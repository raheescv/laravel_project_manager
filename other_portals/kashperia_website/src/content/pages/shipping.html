THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

Considered delivery, worldwide.

Kashperia delivers worldwide. Every order is prepared with care, with delivery services tailored to its destination. For shipments outside Qatar, we use DHL and provide shipment tracking so clients can follow their parcel online. Within Qatar, same-day delivery is available, together with an express delivery option within two hours for eligible orders and delivery locations.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Worldwide delivery with DHL

International orders are dispatched through DHL, with a tracking reference provided once the parcel is handed to the courier. Clients may follow the shipment through DHL’s online tracking service. International transit times depend on destination, customs processing and carrier conditions.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Qatar — same-day delivery

For deliveries within Qatar, same-day service is available for qualifying orders. The applicable order cut-off, product readiness and delivery area will be confirmed at checkout or by Client Services.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Qatar — two-hour express delivery

For occasions when time matters, Kashperia offers an express delivery option within two hours, subject to item availability, delivery address, order confirmation and operational capacity. Eligibility and any express delivery charge will be confirmed before payment.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Dispatch, tracking and assistance

Once an order is dispatched, the relevant delivery confirmation and tracking information will be shared. For international orders, live shipment updates are available through DHL’s tracking platform. Our Customer Service Team can assist with delivery enquiries.

05 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Duties, taxes and delivery charges

Shipping charges, applicable duties and taxes will be communicated before purchase where known. International customs duties, import taxes and clearance delays may depend on the destination country and local authorities.
