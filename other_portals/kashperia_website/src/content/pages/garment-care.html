THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

The art of keeping.

All Kashperia shawls, ghutra and fabrics are dry clean only. Consult a professional cleaner experienced with fine fibres and embroidery; store pieces clean and dry.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

01 / After wearing

Allow your shawl or garment to air naturally in a dry, ventilated space away from direct sunlight, radiators and other sources of heat. Avoid unnecessary friction against jewellery or rough surfaces.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

02 / Folding and hanging

Dry clean only for every Kashperia textile. Do not machine wash, hand wash or tumble dry.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

03 / Storage

Store each piece completely dry in a breathable cover or protective box, away from humidity, prolonged sunlight and compression. Protect natural fibres against moths and avoid heavy items resting on embroidery.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

04 / Professional cleaning

Dry clean only. Choose a reputable specialist familiar with fine cashmere, Shahmina, pashmina, Sea Island cotton, silk, wool and delicate hand embroidery. Do not machine wash, hand wash, tumble dry or apply household stain treatments.

05 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

05 / Travel

Fold gently with tissue or a soft protective layer. Avoid tight packing, sharp creases and sustained pressure on hand-embroidered areas. Unpack and air your piece upon arrival.
