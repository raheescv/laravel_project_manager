THE KASHPERIA GUIDE

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kashperia?

Kashperia is a luxury house specialising in exceptional shawls, fine fabrics, ghutra, shumagh, bespoke creations and considered gifting. Our work draws on Kashmiri textile heritage and specialist craftsmanship in Italy, Switzerland and Kashmir.

What is Shahmina?

Shahmina is Kashperia’s name for exceptionally fine baby pashmina sourced from the Changthang region. Fibre characteristics and composition vary by product; refer to the individual product description and available documentation.

Where are Kashperia creations made?

Origins depend on the collection. Our work involves artisans and specialist textile makers in Kashmir, Italy, Switzerland and the United Kingdom. Each product page provides the relevant origin information where available.

How do I choose a men’s shawl size?

Men’s shawls are offered in sizes 54, 56, 58 and 60. Sea Island White ghutra is available in 52, 54, 56, 58, 60 and 62; Sea Island Shumagh in 55, 58, 60 and 62. These are stated product sizes.

What sizes are women’s shawls?

Women’s shawls are approximately 200 × 100 cm, except Topaz, which measures approximately 70 × 200 cm. Measurements are approximate because of the nature of textile finishing.

What sizes are available for children?

Children’s shawls are available in sizes 46, 48, 50 and 52. Children’s fabric cuts are available in lengths of 2 and 2.5 metres.

How are fabrics sold?

Fabrics are generally sold in pre-cut lengths of 3.50 metres. Selected fabrics may be available in lengths up to 4 metres; please enquire about the specific cloth.

Can I commission a bespoke creation?

Yes. Kashperia offers bespoke consultation for select fabrics, shawls, embroidery, colours and finishing. Feasibility, design, timing and price are confirmed individually by the House.

Can I personalise a product with a monogram?

Selected products may be personalised with hand-embroidered monograms in a choice of fonts and colours. Contact Client Services for availability and approval of your design.

What is the KASHPERIA PRIVÉ?

KASHPERIA PRIVÉ is a private members’ destination for bespoke commissions and rare creations. Access is by introduction or reference from an existing member, or by direct invitation from Kashperia, subject to approval.

What makes a Limited Edition?

Limited Editions are seasonal shawls or fabrics made from rare materials in expressly defined production quantities. Each release identifies the number produced; some unavailable editions may offer a waitlist.

Can I join a waitlist?

Where offered, you may register your interest in an unavailable limited creation. Joining a waitlist is not a reservation or guarantee of future availability.

How does Kashperia authenticate its products?

Each product is accompanied by a form of authentication. Verification may be requested online where supported or by telephone. Selected products also incorporate NFC technology; availability varies by creation.

What is the Kashperia Standard?

Kashperia describes a 30-point shawl inspection framework: six checkpoints each for raw yarn, weaving, colour dyeing, embroidery and finishing. Certified independent laboratories in Italy, the United Kingdom and India are used for applicable material testing, including identity, diameter, composition and coefficient of variation.

How should I care for my Kashperia product?

All Kashperia shawls and fabrics are dry clean only. Use a specialist cleaner familiar with fine fibres and embroidery; follow the care label and store garments carefully.

Does Kashperia ship internationally?

Yes. Kashperia offers worldwide delivery. International shipments outside Qatar can be tracked through DHL using the tracking information provided when dispatched.

What delivery options are available in Qatar?

Same-day delivery is available within Qatar, alongside an express option targeting delivery within two hours for eligible orders and locations. Availability and charges are confirmed at checkout or by Client Services.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Kashperia accepts major credit cards for online purchases. Secure checkout and cardholder authentication depend on the live payment gateway and issuing bank.

Are online payments secure?

Kashperia intends to use an encrypted payment process and recognised card-security safeguards, including 3-D Secure where supported. Do not share one-time passwords or complete card details by email or messaging.

Can I return or exchange an order?

Eligibility depends on the product, its condition, whether it has been personalised, the country of purchase and applicable consumer rights. Consult the Returns & Exchanges page and contact Client Services before returning an item.
