THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

A considered approach.

If you wish to request a return or exchange, contact the Kashperia Customer Service Team with your order reference, product details and reason for the request. The applicable eligibility criteria, deadlines and lawful exceptions will be communicated under the terms in force at the time of purchase.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Eligibility and condition

Where permitted by law, items submitted for return should be unused beyond reasonable inspection, with original labels, accessories and packaging. Proof of purchase may be requested.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Bespoke and personalised pieces

Custom-made, monogrammed or otherwise personalised products may be subject to legally permitted exceptions to ordinary return rights. This does not affect mandatory rights for defective or misdescribed goods.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Incorrect or faulty items

Please notify the Customer Service Team promptly and include photographs and your order details. Your mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Return shipping

The applicable procedure, carrier arrangements and responsibility for return costs will be communicated in accordance with the purchase terms and relevant law.

05 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, it will ordinarily be made to the original payment method unless otherwise lawfully agreed, within the period required by applicable law.

Publication note: This is preliminary editorial information, not an approved legal policy. Final terms, operating entity details and regional consumer-rights disclosures must be confirmed before the website accepts orders.
