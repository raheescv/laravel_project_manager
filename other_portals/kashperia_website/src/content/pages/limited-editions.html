THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

Created in finite numbers. Remembered beyond the season.

Some materials cannot be obtained in abundance, and some forms of craftsmanship cannot be hurried. Kashperia Limited Editions bring these two truths together in exceptional shawls and fabrics, created from rare fibres and finished with an exacting attention to detail. Each collection is produced in a deliberately limited quantity for its season. Rather than relying on an undefined promise of exclusivity, the House identifies the total number of pieces made for each limited release. A creation may be one of a small series or a singular expression, but its edition is always defined by the production record. Once a seasonal edition is fully allocated, further availability cannot be presumed. For selected creations, clients may request to join a waitlist to be considered should a piece become available or a future allocation be offered. A place on the waitlist does not guarantee a purchase or a future edition.
