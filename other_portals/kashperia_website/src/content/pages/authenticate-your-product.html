THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

A signature of authenticity.

Every Kashperia creation is accompanied by a form of authentication appropriate to the product. Depending on the collection, this may include an individual identification number, an authenticity certificate, a distinctive security label or another recognised form of product identification. Clients may request verification through our online authentication service, where available, or directly by telephone with the Kashperia Customer Service Team.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

An individual identity

Authentication details vary by product and collection. Retain the original product label, certificate, identification number or other accompanying documentation for future reference.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Online or personal verification

Where an item is enrolled in our online authentication registry, its unique identifier may be checked through the official Kashperia website. Alternatively, our team can assist by telephone on +974 4490 5899 or +974 3332 2965. Verification may require supporting photographs or purchase information.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

NFC-enabled creations

Selected Kashperia products also incorporate Near Field Communication (NFC) technology. Where present, the embedded chip offers an additional digital point of access to product information or authentication, subject to the relevant verification service. Not all Kashperia creations carry an NFC chip.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Our commitment to provenance

Authentication complements Kashperia’s commitment to traceable materials, responsible documentation and careful craftsmanship. A product identifier or NFC response should be verified against Kashperia’s authorised records; the presence of a tag alone is not proof of authenticity.
