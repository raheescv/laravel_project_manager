THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

The art of a personal creation.

Kashperia makes unrivalled bespoke fabrics, shawls, wraps and scarves. The essence of the bespoke process lies in understanding the clients taste, habits and sensing their wishes. The first step of a bespoke atelier is to offer the client most exclusive and luxurious collection of fabrics.

The fabrics are made on our heritage traditional looms by using age old hand spinning and hand weaving techniques Once the Fabrics are selected, our Italian designers use their artistic flair and technical skills to create designs that are not only in line with the latest fashion trends from around the world but also with reverence to the client’s personality and attitude. Each design is unique and is made explicitly for each client. After finalizing the fabric and the design pattern, the artisans of the bespoke atelier do the most intricate hand embroidery on the fabric based on the approved designs from our Italian design studios. Due to the extremely fine and soft nature of the fabric, it requires the highest level of craftsmanship to do the hand embroidery work

On an average it takes up to three to five months to finish the embroidery on each product depending upon the design complexity. The detailed handwork on the fabric is so perfect that each pattern stands out like a picture and the final product exemplifies the zenith of craftsmanship

The whole process of making the product is done by hand and the finished product is presented in the hand sculpted wooden walnut box.
