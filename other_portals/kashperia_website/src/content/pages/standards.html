THE KASHPERIA STANDARD

30 points of consideration. Five pillars of excellence.

Every shawl is assessed through a 30-point quality framework covering raw yarn, weaving, colour dyeing, embroidery and finishing. Each pillar has six defined checkpoints. The criteria below describe the inspection framework; individual testing procedures may vary by product and material.

Raw Yarn

  1. Confirm the declared fibre type and documented source of the raw material.
  2. Review supplier documentation and traceability of the yarn batch.
  3. Assess fibre fineness and relevant laboratory results where applicable.
  4. Inspect yarn evenness and consistency across the batch.
  5. Check yarn strength and suitability for the intended weave.
  6. Examine cleanliness and identify visible foreign fibres or contamination.

Weaving

  1. Confirm the weave structure and pattern against the approved specification.
  2. Inspect uniformity of the fabric surface and yarn distribution.
  3. Check for broken threads, missed picks, snags and other weaving defects.
  4. Assess the consistency and neatness of selvedges and edges.
  5. Check the shawl dimensions against the relevant product specification.
  6. Assess fabric handle, drape and overall weaving finish.

Colour Dyeing

  1. Compare the finished shade with the approved colour reference.
  2. Inspect colour uniformity across the entire shawl.
  3. Check for visible streaks, patches or uneven dye absorption.
  4. Examine colour consistency between panels, borders and body.
  5. Review applicable colourfastness test results where specified.
  6. Inspect the fabric for staining, transfer or finishing-related discolouration.

Embroidery

  1. Check the embroidered design against the approved artwork or pattern.
  2. Inspect stitch precision, density and consistency.
  3. Confirm placement, symmetry and alignment of embroidered motifs.
  4. Examine thread ends, knots and loose or missed stitches.
  5. Check embroidery colours and thread selection against the approved design.
  6. Assess the fabric beneath embroidery for puckering, damage or distortion.

Finishing

  1. Inspect the final shawl for marks, snags, pulls and surface defects.
  2. Check the quality and consistency of edges, hems and tassels where applicable.
  3. Confirm final measurements and presentation against product specifications.
  4. Assess pressing, softness, drape and overall hand-feel.
  5. Check care labels, product identifiers and authentication materials where applicable.
  6. Complete final visual inspection and confirm packaging readiness.

Kashperia uses certified independent third-party laboratories in Italy, the United Kingdom and India for relevant material assessments, including material identity or DNA-based testing where applicable, fibre diameter, composition and coefficient of variation (CV). Laboratory methods and available reports depend on the material and product.
