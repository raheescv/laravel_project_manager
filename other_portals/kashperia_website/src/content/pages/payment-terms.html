Payment Terms

Confidence in every transaction.

THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

Secure and considered purchasing.

Kashperia accepts all major credit cards for online purchases, subject to successful authorisation by the issuing bank and availability of the live payment gateway. The final amount payable, including applicable delivery charges and taxes, will be presented before order confirmation. Our online payment arrangements are designed to follow applicable Qatar Central Bank requirements and recognised e-commerce security standards.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Accepted credit cards

Online purchases may be made using major credit cards supported by Kashperia’s activated payment gateway. Card acceptance is subject to verification and authorisation by the relevant bank or payment network.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Secure checkout and encrypted transactions

Online card payments should be processed through a secure payment gateway using encrypted connections. The payment service provider must meet applicable Qatar Central Bank requirements and relevant Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) obligations. The specific payment provider and active safeguards will be confirmed at launch.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Cardholder verification

For additional protection, your issuing bank may require 3-D Secure verification, a one-time password (OTP) or another authentication step before approving a payment. Never disclose an OTP, card PIN or password to anyone claiming to represent Kashperia.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Protection of payment information

Sensitive payment-card authentication information, including CVV/CVC and PIN details, must not be stored by Kashperia. Payment credentials should be handled by the authorised payment processor in accordance with applicable card-industry requirements. Kashperia will never request complete card details or OTPs by email, telephone or WhatsApp.

05 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

Pricing, confirmation and assistance

The applicable currency, product price, delivery charges and any additional fees will be shown before payment is completed. An order is subject to successful payment authorisation and acceptance under the applicable terms of sale. For any payment concerns, contact info@kashperia.com or +974 4490 5899.

Publication note: This is preliminary editorial information, not an approved legal policy. Final terms, operating entity details and regional consumer-rights disclosures must be confirmed before the website accepts orders.
