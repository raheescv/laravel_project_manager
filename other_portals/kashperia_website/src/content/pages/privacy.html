THE HOUSE OF KASHPERIA

Privacy and discretion.

At Kashperia, discretion is part of the client experience. This notice explains the principal ways personal information may be handled when visitors browse our website, enquire about collections, request services or place orders through an activated online store. The final published policy must identify the responsible operating entity and reflect the actual website systems and applicable laws.

01 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

01 / Information we collect

Depending on your interaction with Kashperia, we may collect your name, contact details, country, delivery and billing information, correspondence, product preferences, order information and limited technical information about website usage. Payment-card details should be processed by the approved payment provider rather than stored directly by Kashperia.

02 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

02 / How information is used

Information may be used to answer enquiries, process and fulfil orders, arrange deliveries, manage personalisation and after-sales services, maintain client relationships, improve website security and comply with legal obligations. Marketing communications will be sent only where legally permitted.

03 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

03 / Confidentiality and protection

We aim to apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, restricting access to authorised personnel and service providers who require information for legitimate purposes.

04 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

04 / Sharing information

Information may be shared where necessary with authorised operating entities, payment processors, logistics providers, technology suppliers and professional advisers. Kashperia does not intend to sell personal information to advertisers.

05 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

05 / International transfers

Orders and enquiries may involve international partners and service providers. Where personal information is transferred across borders, appropriate safeguards will be applied as required by applicable law.

06 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

06 / Data retention

Personal information should be retained only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected and for applicable legal, accounting or business requirements, then securely deleted or anonymised.

07 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

07 / Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent, object to certain processing or exercise other available data-protection rights through Client Services.

08 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

08 / Cookies and website technology

The website may use essential cookies and, where enabled, analytics or other technologies. Any required notices, consent mechanisms and visitor choices must be implemented before non-essential technologies are activated.

09 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

09 / External websites

Links to independent websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

10 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

10 / Updates to this notice

This notice may be revised to reflect changes in services, website functionality or legal requirements. The current version and effective date should be published on the live website.

11 / THE KASHPERIA MAISON

11 / Privacy enquiries

For questions about the use of your personal information, please contact the Kashperia Customer Service Team using the official contact facilities on this website.

Publication note: This is preliminary editorial information, not an approved legal policy. Final terms, operating entity details and regional consumer-rights disclosures must be confirmed before the website accepts orders.
