A · Workbench. The clutter today is three columns of stacked white cards, each with its own border and shadow, fighting the artwork for attention. Here the rails are plain surfaces divided by hairlines, and the left rail becomes an accordion where one section is open at a time — every collapsed row still shows its live value, so nothing is hidden. The inspector splits into Layout / Type / Barcode tabs, so twelve controls become four at a time. The stage grows into the hero with a ruler, a real drop shadow and a zoom pill. Accent is var(--bs-primary) throughout.

No card borders anywhere; hairlines and whitespace do the separating. The right side is a proof card on tinted stock, the way a print shop hands you a sample. Warmest and calmest of the three; least like a generic admin panel.
