Overview — same data, calmer frame

Shared by all three directions
  • Net sales stays the one hero; Gross and Discount ride inside it instead of taking their own tiles.
  • Six figure tiles, 3-up on tablet and 2-up on phone, each with a colour edge — no more half-empty rows.
  • Payment methods: one stacked share bar over a Method · Txns · Sales · Net table, zero rows muted.
  • By day keeps the bar chart; the best day is gold.
  • Tablet uses two columns so the page ends with the fold instead of a long scroll.
  • The date range lives in the command bar (tablet) / the filter card (phone) — one place, both screens.

Filters & sorting — one surface, both screens

Popover on tablet, bottom sheet on phone
  • Everything that filters a report in one place: range presets, custom from/to, branch, staff, and product type.
  • Sort by becomes explicit: Amount, Quantity, Bills or Name, each with a High→Low / Low→High toggle. Today the list is always "amount, highest first" with no way to say otherwise.
  • Click-and-go: tapping a value applies it and the list rebuilds — no Save button.
  • Applied filters show as removable chips above the list, so you always know what you're looking at.
  • On tablet the same sorts are also one tap away in the table's column headers (direction A).
  • Backend note: the API takes sort=amount|quantity today. Bills/Name and the direction toggle need a small addition to GetRequest + GetAction — I'll do it with the port.

A · Ledger

Quiet chrome, the numbers do the work
  • The three filter rows collapse into one toolbar line in the command bar's sunk-segment language — no more three fat gradient bars.
  • Rows become a real table: rank · name · qty · bills · share · amount, tabular figures lined up down the page.
  • Column headers name each number and double as sort buttons (the live one is tinted with an arrow).
  • Rank 1 gets a small gold numeral instead of a tinted card plus a TOP badge.
  • Grand total on a footer bar with the page counter beside it.
  • Phone drops Qty/Bills columns and moves the share bar under the name.

B · Podium

Best sellers first, the tail underneath
  • Same one-line toolbar, then the top three on a podium: #1 as a brand card with amount, avg ticket and share.
  • Everything after rank 3 is a quiet two-column list on tablet, one column on phone — the width finally gets used.
  • Reads like a leaderboard; strongest for By Staff, still fine for items.
  • Grand total on the list footer.

C · Analyst

Filters + summary rail, list on the right
  • Tablet splits: a left rail holds the breakdown/rank/type filters stacked with row counts, plus a share donut and the totals.
  • The list on the right is only the list — full height, no chrome above it, so roughly twice as many rows fit.
  • The donut answers "how concentrated are my sales?" (top 3 = 69%) at a glance.
  • Phone falls back to chips + list, so only tablet gains the rail.
