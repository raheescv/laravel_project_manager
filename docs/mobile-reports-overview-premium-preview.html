A · Hero Ledger

One headline, everything else supports it
  • Net sales is the headline in large type, with Gross and Discount right under it, and a success ring beside it.
  • Invoices / Avg ticket / Returns sit in one inset strip.
  • A "Sales mix" bar splits Products vs Services; Item total rides on the section line.
  • Payment methods become one list with a share bar each; a method with no sales dims instead of taking a whole card.
  • No saturated gradient tiles, so the card reads calm in every preset.

B · Refined Tiles

The tiles you know, made legible
  • Net sales becomes one wide brand tile with Gross and Discount beside it.
  • The rest are soft tinted tiles with a colour edge instead of full gradients, and full labels that never truncate.
  • Values show the full amount (QAR 1,322.00), not QAR1.32K.
  • Payment methods collapse to a stacked share bar plus a compact Method · Txns · Sales · Net table.

C · Executive

Brand hero panel + calm statement
  • A gradient hero panel in the preset colours holds Net sales plus six glass chips (Gross, Discount, Avg ticket, Invoices, Returns, Item total).
  • Below, a statement card: Sales mix and Payments as clean rows with a share bar and percentage.
  • The most "premium" of the three; matches the Day Session hero.
