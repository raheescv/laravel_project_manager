Fixture Comments — Rent-Out Checklist

What this is. A per-area Fixture Comments block that sits directly below each category's items — on the Checklist tab (editable), on the checklist print/sign page (read-only + owner signature), and in the PDF. Each area holds multiple entries; each entry carries a before photo, an after photo, comments, a completion status and an optional completion date. The owner signs once per area to accept that area's work.
