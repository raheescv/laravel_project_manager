Category rail · with image / without image

Shipped. All four are live as Settings → Sale Configuration → Category display in the app — device-local, like “Products per row” beside it, so a photo rail on the counter tablet does not force one onto a manager’s phone. “Name only” is the default and is exactly today’s rail. Everything above the hairline is the fixed head, so a taller rail is a permanently taller head — that trade is why each option carries its height.

Each row draws the rail it selects, at its real height — the list is its own preview, and applies on tap with no Save button.
What it costs, per option
  • Name only — 36pt rail. The default, and the state the screen has always been in.
  • Name with photo — 42pt rail (+6pt). Cheapest “with image”: the rail keeps its shape and a photo is a hint, not the subject. Best when only some categories have photos.
  • Photo card — 84pt rail (+48pt). Photos become the way you pick — good for food and retail where staff recognise the picture faster than the word. Costs most of a product-tile row.
  • Photo tile — 66pt rail (+30pt). Boldest. Needs consistently good, dark-enough photos or the white name over a pale pastry reads badly — the scrim helps, it doesn’t save it.
