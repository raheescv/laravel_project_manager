What's wrong now

The label row is justify-content: space-between, so in a 290 px column the two icons get thrown to the far right edge — ~150 px of dead gap between "Customer" and the controls that act on it. They read as belonging to the column, not to the select underneath. The eye is also disabled until a customer is picked, so on a fresh ticket it looks broken rather than inactive.
