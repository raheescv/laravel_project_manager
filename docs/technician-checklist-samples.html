FixMate · Hand-over Checklist

Three directions for bringing the RentOut move-in / move-out checklist into the technician app: the checklists assigned to you, a photo, status and comment for every item, then hand-over signatures for each role. All four screens in each direction work. Tap the status buttons, take photos, sign the pads, and switch between light and dark.

A · Walkthrough

The technician walks the unit and the app walks with them. Each item gets one full card: take the photo, tap a big status button, add a note with one tap, then Next. Rooms run along the top so they can jump around. A review screen catches anything they skipped before signing.

  • Big buttons for one-handed use while holding the phone up to the item
  • The master item's reference photo appears in the corner of the capture area
  • Quick comments: Scratched, Not working, Missing, Stained…
  • Signatures as stacked role cards. The lessee signs on this device.

B · Inspection Sheet

The web checklist tab, reworked for a phone. Items are grouped by room in collapsible sections, each row has a ✓ ✗ — toggle, and each room has a "Mark all good" button. Experienced technicians can finish a 40-item villa in a couple of minutes. On a move-out each row also shows its move-in status. Tap a row to compare the move-in photo with the new one.

  • Fastest for large units, and close to how the web tab works
  • Move-In and Move-Out switch on one sheet, with the move-in status shown on each row
  • Damage cost appears only for items marked Damaged on a move-out
  • Sample uses a Lease, so roles read Site Engineer / Admin Coordinator

"Assigned" and role rules

Proposal: a technician sees rent-outs where they are the facility_coordinator_id or leasing_coordinator_id. They can sign their own role(s) with their account, and can capture the Lessee signature in person on their device. Any other staff role is shown locked with the assigned person's name.
