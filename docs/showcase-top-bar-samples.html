Top bar — three arrangements

Back, mark, in stock, branch, overflow. Shown on the funnel root (no back control) and an inner step (back plus breadcrumbs), because those are the two shapes the bar has to hold — and at 320pt, 402pt and tablet, because the narrow phone is where every one of these either works or does not.

All three fix the same two things the bar has today: mixed heights (the mark was 40 against 38pt controls) and ad-hoc spacing (12, 4, 8 with no system). Everything below is 38pt tall on an 8pt gap, with 14pt between groups. The branch name is the longest real one, and it is the only element allowed to shrink.
