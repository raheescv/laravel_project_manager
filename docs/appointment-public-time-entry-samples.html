All three are the same hybrid — the month calendar from Open Hours, the generated fixed slots you have today, and a typed From/To window — arranged three different ways. The slots stay exactly what the server produces now (open hours ÷ the config slot length, minus what is taken and inside the notice window); typing is the escape hatch for anyone the grid does not suit.

What the server needs either way. A typed window is not on the grid, so booking validates a window: inside that day's open hours, not overlapping an existing appointment, past the minimum-notice cut-off, end after start. The appointment gains a real ends_at, so a customer who asks for 90 minutes gets 90 minutes. A tapped slot is just a pre-filled window — one code path, not two.
Hybrid H1 · recommended

Presets & Window — the slots fill the fields

Both paths are visible at once and neither is a mode. The generated slots sit where they always did; tapping one writes it into the From/To fields underneath, and typing over those fields simply un-picks the chip. The window is the single source of truth, so there is one thing to submit and one thing to validate.

Nothing is hidden behind a toggle, and the customer who just wants "10 o'clock" is still one tap from done. This is the one I would build.

Rail — the day drawn, slots as the quick way in

Calendar, then the chosen day as one bar: open hours tinted, booked stretches hatched, and the customer's window drawn solid and moving live as the fields change. The fixed slots sit under the rail as one-tap presets. Nobody has to imagine what "10:00 – 11:00" means against a busy diary — they can see it.
