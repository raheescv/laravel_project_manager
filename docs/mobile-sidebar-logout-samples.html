Where does “Log out” live in the sidebar?

The phone drawer already ends in a flat red Log out row; the tablet side rail has none at all — on a tablet there is no other way out of the app. Four options for the rail (A–D) and two refinements for the drawer footer (E–F). Pick one from each row, or one overall.

Identity puck → menu
