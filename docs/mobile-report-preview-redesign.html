A · Studio Dock

Branded header · floating paper switch · one dock
  • A header in the brand gradient names the report and the range. The report chips sit inside it and switch the page in place.
  • The A4 / Thermal switch floats over the page and stays in reach while you scroll a long roll. It only appears for reports that have a roll (Overview, Session).
  • The bottom dock holds one thing: a full-width Print that says where it goes (the paired printer, or "Choose a printer").
  • Share (the phone's share sheet: WhatsApp, email, Drive…) and Save are two small icons in the header.
  • Closest to the app's current look (gradient heroes, frosted cards).

B · Clean Toolbar

Quiet app bar · underline tabs · labelled action bar
  • A plain white bar keeps the attention on the paper. Share and Save are two small icons at the top right.
  • Reports are underline tabs, like a document viewer.
  • The bottom bar pairs the paper switch (A4 | 80 mm) with Print, so you choose the paper right where you print.
  • The most familiar pattern.

C · Floating Glass

Full-bleed page · every control in thumb reach
  • The page runs edge to edge under frosted glass bars, so the most paper is visible.
  • The bottom panel holds the choices and the one action: the report picker (icon over label, same as today's), the paper switch, then a full-width Print.
  • Sending moves to two small icons at the top right. Share opens the phone's share sheet, which already has WhatsApp, email and Drive (and Save to Files on iPhone). Save puts a copy in Downloads on Android.
  • With no send row, the panel is shorter and more of the page shows.
Try it: tap a report, then flip A4 ⇄ Thermal. The page is rebuilt behind the same "Preparing thermal print…" card the app shows. By Item / By Category / By Staff are A4 only, so the paper switch hides for them.
