A · Classic Legal: serif type and black ink only, no boxes or shading. Hairline rules and a centre divider, like a lawyer-drafted contract. B · Official Register: a ruled grid like the client's Word draft, with the article number in a centre column and double-ruled headings. C · Contemporary: sans-serif type in one navy ink, article numbers in the margin, and party details as label/value pairs. None of them uses grey fills, which print blotchy on bond paper. The hatched bands mark the space left blank for the pre-printed letterhead and footer.
