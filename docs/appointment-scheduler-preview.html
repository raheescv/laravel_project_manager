06 · Settings → Viewing Emails — a new tab in the settings shell (resources/views/settings/index.blade.php), guarded by configuration.settings. Templates are typed, exactly one may be active per type, and the body uses the existing <x-rich-text-editor> with App\Support\RichText sanitising on save. No tenant wording ships in PHP — the starter body is neutral and the tenant pastes their own.
