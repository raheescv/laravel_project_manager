Sample A

Command Rail

Filters live permanently in a left rail — every salesman with their appointment count, statuses, legend — so the calendar never loses screen to a filter bar. Clicking an event slides a detail drawer in from the right; the calendar stays visible and keeps its selection, so you can walk through several appointments without a modal blocking the grid. On a phone the rail becomes a slide-over and the drawer becomes a bottom sheet.

Click any event in the grid to open the drawer. Try the view segments and the phone toggle.

Sample B

Glass Studio

Maximum grid: the calendar runs edge to edge and the only chrome is one floating glass toolbar carrying the date nav, view segments and filter chips. Clicking an event opens a popover pinned to the event itself — Google-Calendar style — so your eye never leaves the slot you clicked. On a phone the popover becomes a bottom sheet. Fewest pixels spent on chrome; filters are chips rather than a full rail, so it suits a team of a handful of salesmen.

Click any event — the popover anchors to it.

Sample C

Split Board

The operations view. A KPI strip across the top (today, this week, awaiting, no-shows), the calendar in the middle, and a permanent right panel that shows the selected appointment — or, when nothing is selected, what's coming up next. Nothing ever slides over the grid, and the panel is always there to read, which suits a screen someone leaves open all day. Costs the most horizontal space; on a phone the panel stacks under the calendar.

Click an event to fill the right panel; it falls back to "Up next" when you close it.
