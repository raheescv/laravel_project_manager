QLOUD POS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short
  • QLOUD POS is a business app. It is used by staff of businesses that subscribe to the QLOUD POS platform.
  • We collect only what is needed to run point-of-sale and back-office features.
  • We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Biometric data (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint) stays on your device. We never receive it.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Astra (“we”, “us”, “our”), the developer of the QLOUD POS app for iOS, iPadOS, Android and macOS (the “App”), handles information when you use the App.

The App connects to a QLOUD POS account operated by a business, such as a shop, restaurant, school canteen or property office (the “Business”). The Business decides what data is entered into its account and who may access it. For that business data, the Business is the data controller and we process it on the Business’s behalf.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and sign-in information

  • Your name, username or email address, phone number and the role and branch your Business assigns to you.
  • Your password and quick-sign-in PIN. These are sent over an encrypted connection and stored only in encrypted or hashed form.
  • A sign-in token, kept in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore) so you stay signed in.

2.2 Profile photo (optional)

If you choose to set a profile photo, you can take one with the camera or pick one from your photo library. Only the photo you select is uploaded. The App does not read or upload anything else from your library.

2.3 Business and transaction data

When you use the App for your work, it handles data that belongs to the Business, including:

  • Sales, returns, payments, receipts, day sessions and reports.
  • Products, prices, stock levels and product images.
  • Customer details the Business records, such as name and phone number.
  • For schools that use student cards: the card identifier and the student’s account balance needed to complete a purchase.

2.4 Technical information

  • App version, device type and operating system, used to keep the App compatible and to troubleshoot problems.
  • Server logs of requests made to the QLOUD POS service, such as time, IP address and the action performed. These are used for security, auditing and support.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, browsing history or health data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Device permissions

The App asks for permissions only when a feature needs them. You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings; the related feature will then stop working, but the rest of the App will continue to work.

4. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To provide the App’s features: selling, printing receipts, managing stock, viewing reports and similar tasks.
  • To keep the till working offline and sync sales once a connection returns.
  • To keep audit records of who did what, as required by the Business.
  • To provide support, fix problems and improve reliability.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. Data stored on your device

To work quickly and offline, the App keeps some data on your device:

  • A copy of the product catalogue and product images.
  • Sales made while offline, until they are synced to the server.
  • Your App settings and preferences.
  • Your sign-in token, in secure storage.

This data is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

6. Sharing of information

We share information only in these cases:

  • With your Business. Authorised administrators of the Business can see the data in its account, including activity by staff users.
  • Service providers. Hosting and infrastructure providers that store and run the QLOUD POS service for us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Payment providers. If the Business uses card payments or online top-ups, payment details are handled directly by the payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, court order or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the Business or us.

7. Data security

  • All communication between the App and the server uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords and PINs are never stored in plain text.
  • Each Business’s data is kept separate from other Businesses on the platform.
  • Access within a Business is limited by roles and permissions set by its administrators.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information.

8. Data retention

We keep business and transaction data for as long as the Business keeps its QLOUD POS account active, or as long as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes. When a Business closes its account, its data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period, except where the law requires us to keep it.

9. Your rights and account deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, or to object to certain processing.

  • Staff accounts are created and managed by your Business. To change or delete your account, contact your Business administrator first.
  • You can also email us at info@technoastra.com with the subject “Account deletion” or “Privacy request”. We will verify your identity, work with your Business where needed, and respond within 30 days.
  • You can edit your own name, phone, email and profile photo from the Profile screen in the App.

Some records, such as completed sales and financial entries, may need to be kept by the Business for legal or accounting reasons even after your account is deleted. In that case your personal details are removed or anonymised where possible.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is meant for business staff and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the App. Where a school uses student cards, student information is provided and controlled by the school, and only the details needed to complete a purchase are shown in the App.

11. International transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than the one you live in. When this happens, we take steps to make sure it remains protected in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable law, including Qatar’s Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the App or by other reasonable means.
